# Fitness Check-In Template

Use this when the athlete asks what to do today.

## Ask If Missing

- Any soreness or pain today?
- How much time do you have?
- Did you sleep okay?
- Did you train yesterday? If yes, what feels worked?

## Recommend

- Session type
- 4-6 exercises max
- Sets, reps, and starting weights based on recent logs
- One clear progression target
- One safety or form note

## After Workout, Capture

- What exercises actually happened?
- Weight, reps, and sets
- What felt too easy or too hard?
- Any pain?
- Protein hit / close / low
- Creatine taken / missed
